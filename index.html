About us

Hishiro is an Indonesian streetwear brand founded in 2021 in Semarang that blends Japanese-inspired aesthetics and anime influences with contemporary silhouettes like hoodies, jackets, raw denim, and cargo pants. Designed around the concept of stepping into your own "main character moment," the label emphasizes identity and storytelling through cultural collections available on the Hishiro official website and through curated stockists like Wormhole Store.

Philosophy

Hishiro, builds its philosophy around the slogan “The World is Watching. Make them Remember” to empower individuals to feel like the main character in their own story. The label treats anime fashion as a respected cultural art form rather than a niche hobby, focusing on wearable art that blends Japanese pop culture, contemporary silhouettes, and traditional elements into unique designs that break conventional gender norms and remove local stigmas surrounding the genre.

Collections

Hishiro has gained massive popularity for blending Japanese cultural aesthetics and anime narratives with high-quality contemporary streetwear shapes. Famous for their meticulously crafted button-ups, raw denim, and outerwear accented with distinct hardware like fireman clasps, Hishiro launches their apparel through dedicated thematic collections.

Culture

Hishiro fuses Japanese anime culture with modern contemporary fashion.
